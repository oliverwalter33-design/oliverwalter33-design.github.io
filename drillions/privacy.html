Privacy policy

for the app “Drillions” · Last updated: November 2026

1. Controller

WaltHorizon Studios, owner Oliver Walter, Aschaffenburg, Germany. The full postal address and email address are in our legal notice.

2. In short

  • The app does not collect any personal data and sends nothing to us or to third parties.
  • You don’t need an account. We don’t run any servers.
  • Your save game is stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store. We never see payment details.

3. Save game and settings

Your progress and settings (such as language and sound) are stored only in the app’s storage on your device and are not transferred to us. Depending on your device settings they may be included in your own device backup (e.g. iCloud); we have no access to it. You can remove your save game at any time by uninstalling the app.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app verifies purchases on the device using Apple’s own tools. We do not receive payment details, names or email addresses. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

5. App Store

Apple’s privacy policy applies to downloads and updates. Apple only provides us with aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of downloads, crash reports if you have allowed sharing them).

6. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete messages once they are resolved and no legal retention periods apply.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Just write to the email address in our legal notice.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Ours is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (www.lda.bayern.de).

8. Children

The game is not specifically aimed at children. It collects no data from children either.

9. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
