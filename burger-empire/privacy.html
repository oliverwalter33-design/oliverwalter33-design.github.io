Privacy policy

for the app “Burger Empire: Idle Restaurant” · Last updated: November 2026 · The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

Oliver Walter, WaltHorizon Studios, Schweinheimer Straße 79, 63743 Aschaffenburg, Germany
Email: walthorizon@gmx.de

2. Summary

  • No user account is needed. We do not run our own server and never receive your save game.
  • Your save game is stored only on your device.
  • Ads (optional reward videos) are served by Google AdMob. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see payment details.
  • To prevent cheating via the device clock, the app looks up the current time online.

3. Save game and settings

Game progress, language and sound settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device and are not sent to us. Depending on your device settings they may be included in your own device backup (e.g. iCloud or Google account), which we cannot access. You can remove your save game at any time by uninstalling the app.

4. Time lookup

Offline earnings and daily rewards depend on time. So that nobody gains an advantage by changing the device clock, the app fetches the current time over an encrypted connection from servers of Cloudflare, Inc., Google and Apple when it starts and occasionally while you play. For technical reasons your IP address and usual connection data (time, technical request identifier) are transmitted to the respective provider. No game data, identifiers or advertising IDs are sent, and we do not receive this data ourselves.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in fair play (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The providers may also process data in the USA; they are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Advertising with Google AdMob

On request, the app shows reward videos. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

Data processed: IP address, device type, operating system, language setting, approximate location (derived from the IP address), interactions with ads and, on Android devices, the advertising ID. On iPhone the app does not ask for permission to track you across apps; the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used there.

Purposes: delivering and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and – only with your consent – personalising ads.

Legal basis: On first launch we ask for your consent in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Without consent only non-personalised or limited ads are shown.

Withdrawal: You can change your choice at any time in the game’s settings (gear icon) under “Privacy settings (ads)”.

Transfers to third countries: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR).

More: Google privacy policy · How Google uses data from partner apps

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive no payment details, name or email address. The app verifies your purchases directly through the store’s interface on your device and remembers locally which purchases have already been credited. Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. App Store and Google Play

The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply to downloads and updates. The stores only provide us with aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of downloads, crash reports if you have allowed sharing them).

8. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete messages once they are settled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

9. Storage period

Data on your device remains until you uninstall the app. Google, Apple and Cloudflare apply their own retention periods.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Just email us at walthorizon@gmx.de.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Ours is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (www.lda.bayern.de).

11. Children

The game is not specifically aimed at children. Persons under 16 may only consent to personalised ads with their parents’ permission.

12. Changes

If the app or the legal situation changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
