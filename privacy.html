Privacy policy

for the app “Bunker Tycoon” · Last updated: November 2026 · The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

Oliver Walter, WaltHorizon Studios, Schweinheimer Straße 79, 63743 Aschaffenburg, Germany
Email: walthorizon@gmx.de

2. Summary

  • No user account is needed. We do not run our own server and never receive your save game.
  • Your save game is stored only on your device.
  • Ads (optional reward videos) are served by Google AdMob. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see payment details.

3. Save game and settings

Game progress, language and sound settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device and are not sent to us. Depending on your device settings they may be included in your own device backup (e.g. iCloud or your Google account); we have no access to it. You can delete your save game at any time in the menu (“Delete save game”) or by uninstalling the app.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules reminders (e.g. “The expedition is back”) locally on your device. No data is sent to us or third parties. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app menu or in your device settings.

5. Advertising with Google AdMob

The app shows optional reward videos. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

Data processed: your device’s advertising ID (IDFA or Android advertising ID), IP address, device type, operating system, language, approximate location (derived from the IP address) and interactions with ads.

Purposes: serving and measuring ads, limiting frequency, preventing fraud and – only with consent – personalising ads.

Legal basis: On first start we ask for your consent in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). On iPhone the system additionally asks whether the app may track you across apps of other companies (App Tracking Transparency). Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown.

Withdrawal: You can change your choice at any time in the app menu under “Ad privacy settings”, and on iPhone under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Transfers to third countries: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (EU adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR).

More: Google privacy policy · How Google uses data from partner apps

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive payment details, your name or your email address.

To verify, unlock and restore purchases we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). This processes a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID, receipts and transaction data (product, time, price, currency, country) and technical data (platform, app version). The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified. More: RevenueCat privacy policy

7. App Store and Google Play

Downloads and updates are subject to Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies. The stores only give us aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of downloads, crash reports if you allowed sharing them).

8. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete messages once they are resolved and no legal retention obligations apply.

9. Retention

Data on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google’s and RevenueCat’s retention periods apply to data held by them.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Just email us at walthorizon@gmx.de.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, www.lda.bayern.de).

11. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. Persons under 16 may only consent to personalised advertising with their parents’ approval.

12. Changes

We will update this policy if the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
