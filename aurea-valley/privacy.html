Privacy policy

for the app “Aurea Valley” · Last updated: November 2026 · The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

WaltHorizon Studios, owner Oliver Walter, Aschaffenburg, Germany. The full postal address and email address are in our legal notice.

2. Summary

  • No user account is needed. We do not run our own server and never receive your save game.
  • Your save game is stored only on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob unless you bought “No ads”. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see payment details. We use RevenueCat to restore purchases.
  • To prevent cheating with the device clock, the app fetches the current time from the internet.
  • Reminders (“Your harvest is ready”) are scheduled only on your device.

3. Save game and settings

Game progress and settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device and are not sent to us. Depending on your device settings they may be included in your own device backup (e.g. iCloud or your Google account); we have no access to it. You can remove your save game at any time by uninstalling the app.

4. Fetching the current time

Harvests, production and daily rewards depend on time. So nobody gains an advantage by changing the device clock, the app fetches the current time from servers run by Google and Apple over an encrypted connection when it starts and occasionally while playing. For technical reasons your IP address and usual connection data are sent to the provider. No game data, identifiers or advertising IDs are sent, and we do not receive this data.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in fair play (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The providers may process data in the USA; they are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Advertising with Google AdMob

The app shows optional reward videos and occasional ad breaks. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Buying “No ads” removes the ad breaks.

Data processed: IP address, device type, operating system, language, approximate location (derived from the IP address), interactions with ads and – only if you allow it – your device’s advertising ID.

Purposes: delivering and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and – only with consent – ad personalisation.

Legal basis: On first launch we ask for your consent in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland using Google’s consent form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). On iPhone, Apple additionally asks whether the app may track you across apps; the advertising ID (IDFA) is only used if you tap “Allow”. Without consent only non-personalised or limited ads are shown.

Withdrawal: You can change your choice at any time in the game under Options → “Privacy settings” and in your iPhone’s tracking settings.

Transfers to third countries: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR).

More: Google privacy policy · How Google uses data from partner apps

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive payment details, your name or your email address.

To verify purchases, manage the VIP pass and restore purchases we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). This involves a randomly generated user ID, the store’s purchase receipts, device type, operating system, app version and country. Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). More: RevenueCat privacy policy

7. Reminders

If you allow it, the game reminds you about things like ripe harvests. These reminders are scheduled only on your device; no data is sent to us or third parties. You can turn them off in the game’s options or in your phone’s settings.

8. App Store and Google Play

Downloads and updates are subject to Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies. The stores only give us aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of downloads, crash reports if you allowed sharing them).

9. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete it once it is resolved and no legal retention obligations apply.

10. Retention

Data on your device remains until you uninstall the app. Data held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat is subject to their retention periods.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Just email us at the address in our legal notice.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Ours is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (www.lda.bayern.de).

12. Children

The game is not directed at children. Users under 16 may only consent to personalised advertising with their parents’ permission.

13. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we update this policy. The version published here applies.
